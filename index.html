Dr. Dan Lyons

Chiropractor

Dr. Dan Lyons is a lifelong Wisconsin native. After completing undergraduate degrees in Biology and Biochemistry, he attended Palmer College of Chiropractic and graduated in 1996. He opened his first office in his hometown and relocated to Green Bay in 2005. He teaches technique and analysis seminars and workshops around the country as well as in Europe and Japan, writes for a chiropractic journal, and sits on the board of three chiropractic non-profit organizations.
