Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Our Commitment

Precision Chiropractic is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. We strive to provide a positive browsing experience for all visitors and are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website.

Accessibility Standards

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA standards. These guidelines provide a framework for making web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

Measures We Take

Our efforts to ensure accessibility include:

  • Using semantic HTML markup for proper document structure
  • Providing descriptive alternative text for images
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds
  • Supporting keyboard navigation throughout the site
  • Designing responsive layouts that work across devices and screen sizes
  • Using clear, readable fonts and appropriate text sizing
  • Respecting user preferences for reduced motion

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some content on our site may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and resolve any accessibility barriers. Third-party content or tools integrated into our website may not fully meet accessibility standards, and we are working with our vendors to address these issues.

Feedback and Assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our website or need assistance accessing any information, please contact us. We welcome your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address your needs.
