Prenatal Care in Green Bay

The short answer

Prenatal chiropractic care is gentle, low-force care shaped around a changing body. As posture and weight shift through pregnancy, the low back, hips, and pelvis take on new strain. Careful adjustments and comfortable positioning help ease that strain, so you can move, sleep, and rest more comfortably through every trimester and after birth.

Pregnancy changes how your body carries weight, and the low back and pelvis feel it first. Prenatal chiropractic care is one gentle way to stay more comfortable through it. Here is what the care is, who it is for, how it can help, and exactly what a visit looks like.

Who is prenatal chiropractic care for?

It is for anyone feeling the aches of a changing body during pregnancy, or working to stay ahead of them. Care is always matched to the person in front of us, so it is gentle enough for a first pregnancy and familiar territory for parents who have been here before. Many people also come in during recovery after birth.

Parents often come in for
  • Low back ache that builds through the day
  • Hip and pelvic discomfort
  • Sciatic-type aching down the leg
  • Trouble getting comfortable to sleep
  • Postpartum recovery and realignment
Good times to start
  • Any trimester, whenever aches begin
  • Early on, to stay ahead of the strain
  • The busy third trimester, for comfort
  • After birth, for recovery
  • Whenever pregnancy starts reshaping your day

How can it help during pregnancy?

Prenatal care works on comfort and movement. Gentle adjustments help keep the joints of the low back, hips, and pelvis moving well as your body changes, which takes pressure off the tissue that is working overtime. Alongside the hands-on care, you get simple positioning and movement tips so the comfort holds between visits.

  • Ease common low-back and pelvic aches that pregnancy tends to bring.
  • Keep the hips and pelvis moving comfortably as your center of gravity shifts.
  • Rest and sleep a little easier, with positions that take the load off.
  • Gentle support after birth, as your body settles back.

Care is about comfort and movement, not promises. We will always give you an honest sense of whether it is helping and adjust as your pregnancy moves along.

What is a prenatal visit like?

1

We start with where you are

Your trimester, what is aching, how you are sleeping and moving. We listen first, every visit.
2

You are positioned comfortably

Pregnancy pillows and an adjusted table mean you are never lying on your belly. Comfort comes first.
3

Gentle, low-force care

Adjustments are matched to your body and your trimester. Light and specific, never forceful.
4

Simple tips to take home

A few positions and gentle stretches for between visits, so the comfort lasts.

Nearly half of pregnant people have low back pain by the third trimester, and it climbs steadily as pregnancy goes on. If your back is talking to you, you are in very good company, and it is one of the most common reasons parents come in.

Source: The global prevalence of low back pain in pregnancy, a 2023 systematic review and meta-analysis (BMC / NCBI). ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Getting started, and coordinating with your provider

No referral is needed to book, and we are glad to coordinate with your OB or midwife so everyone is on the same page. If chiropractic care is not the right fit for your pregnancy, we will tell you honestly.

  • No referral needed to book a first visit.
  • Happy to coordinate with your OB or midwife.
  • We go over the costs with you before anything begins, so there are no surprises.

We cannot promise a specific outcome, and prenatal care is always a complement to your maternity care, never a replacement for it. Anything that needs a medical provider, we will point you to first.
