When a bone in your spine (vertebra) shifts out of its normal position, it starts to interfere with the nerves and whatever is on the end of that nerve does not work properly. Chiropractic is all about helping you heal from these shifts and perform as well as you can. When other conditions exist we compliment and support many medical therapies.
